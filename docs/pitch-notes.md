# NewGen City — pitch notes

## The problem

Residents often do not know where to report local issues, while city teams
receive duplicate requests with limited visibility into response times.

## The solution

NewGen City gives residents one place to submit an issue and gives city teams a
shared queue that routes reports by category, flags duplicates, tracks service
deadlines, and requires evidence before marking a case resolved.

## Demo flow

1. Submit a report with a category and location.
2. Show the automatic department assignment and SLA deadline.
3. Show duplicate detection for a matching active report.
4. Move a report through the operations queue and record resolution evidence.
5. Review city-wide totals, overdue cases, and the case timeline.
